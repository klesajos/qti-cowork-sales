# Quanti intro deck: slide index

File: `decks/Quanti_intro_2026_CZ.pptx` in the workshop folder (48 slides, 43 MB; built on the same
Quanti Theme). It is the **approved company story**: facts, services, technologies, certifications,
references, offices. Use it two ways:

- **Copy slides, don't rewrite them.** For „Kdo jsme“, „Co umíme“, references or „Kontakt“ in a
  client deck, duplicate the slide from this file (`add_slide.py <deck> slideN.xml -o out.pptx` works
  across files only after you unpack both; simplest is to open the intro deck, delete the slides you
  don't need, and append your own). Pick at most 3–5 reference slides that match the client's
  industry.
- **Facts for text.** Numbers and names below may be used in any client material; they are in
