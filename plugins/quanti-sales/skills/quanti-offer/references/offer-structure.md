# Quanti offer structure

1. **Titulní strana**: purple, white logo, client name, offer name, date, version, offer number.
2. **Shrnutí (1 strana)**: the client's goal in their words, what we propose, price range, time,
   the one reason to choose us. A busy reader stops here.
3. **Jak rozumíme zadání**: the problem, constraints, success metrics. Quote the brief.
4. **Navrhované řešení**: scope by deliverable, not by technology. What is in, what is out.
5. **Postup a harmonogram**: phases with milestones and what the client must supply.
6. **Tým**: roles (and names only if confirmed), what each does on this project.
7. **Cena**: table from the rate card: role, MD, rate, total; sum excl. VAT, VAT, total incl.
   VAT. State assumptions and what changes the price.
8. **Proč Quanti**: 2–3 references relevant to this client, only from approved claims.
9. **Další kroky a platnost nabídky**: who does what next, validity (default 30 days).

Payment and legal terms come from the salesperson; do not invent them.
